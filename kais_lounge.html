how to make a pet that moves

  1. pick a starting pointstart blank, or open an example pet (further down) to see how it's made. examples are the easiest way to learn!
  2. draw frame 1pencil B draws, eraser E (or right-click) erases, bucket G fills, dropper I grabs a color. mirror M draws both sides at once. draw it facing right, it flips by itself.
  3. make frame 2hit duplicate to copy frame 1, then change a little: move the legs, flap the wings, squish it. onion skin O shows the last frame faintly. 2–4 frames is plenty.
  4. watch it playthe preview loops your frames. too fast or slow? drag the speed slider. 4–8 fps feels cute for pixel pets.
  5. choose how it moveswalk, hop, fly, float or follow the mouse. watch it in the little room and pick a size.
  6. name it & saveit shows up on your site right away: in petscape and the 🐾 button.
heads up: pets aren't saved for keeps yet right now they're stored on the server's disk, and Render wipes that every time the site restarts. ask xotiic to run supabase_setup.sql in Supabase once and your pets will stay forever.
